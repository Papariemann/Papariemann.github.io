Another blog post? So soon? Mayhaps Christmas has come early! Of course, Christmas has its winners and losers. The losers of this Christmas get, instead of coal, a whole load more abstract nonsense. Or..they will. I don't have time to write a full mathematical blog so soon after the Hochschild one. 
The winners, however, the good kids to Santa's eyes, will hopefully over the next month or two, see some articles on...

- The pullback and pushforward functors $$f_*$$ and $$f^*$$ of sheaves and their derived functors   

- Spectral Sequences (yes, I apologise, but I have to cover this sooner or later, and it forces me to actually learn about them instead of procrastinating on them for the 50th time)

- An introduction to Triangulated and Derived Categories 

- An introduction to (not necessarily moduli) Stacks Part 1 

- An introduction to (not necessarily moduli, but now Deligne-Mumford) Stacks Part 2 

- An introduction to (not necessarily moduli, not even Deligne-Mumford, but now Artin) Stacks Part 3 

Wait, I am not done just yet. All this is $1$-categorical and all. Ideally, we should *man up* and start Lurie-maxxing. What this concretely means is the following:

- An article on the little n-cubes $\infty$-operad

- An associated article on $E_n$ and especially $E_\infty$-algebras

- The $(\infty, 1)$-Category $\mathrm{CAlg(Sp)}$ and a..slow.. Introduction to Spectral Algebraic Geometry

Emphasis on slow since we do not have an appropriate notion of sheaf for the $(\infty,1)$-setting yet. So...

- An article on $(\infty,1)$-Sheaves and $(\infty, 1)$ Sheaf Topoi so that we can do proper Spectral Algebraic Geometry

- Finally..Spectral Schemes and Deligne-Mumford Stacks, done properly.

- Finally finally, the Moduli Stack of Elliptic Curves and the sheaf $\mathcal{O}^\mathrm{top}$

- Finally, finally, finally, $\mathrm{TMF}$.

Yes, I am in fact fully aware about how outrageous the scope of this is, especially that I am saying this is the plan for the next month or two, especially considering my post publication schedule. But I'll try.
Then maybe sometime later down the line we can slow down all the $(\infty,1)$-categorical slop and appreciate the one and only universal presentable $(\infty, 1)$-category $\mathrm{Sp}$ and its generalised (co)homology theories.
